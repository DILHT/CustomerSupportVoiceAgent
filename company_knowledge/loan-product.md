# Mgodi Personal Loan

## Overview

The Mgodi Personal Loan is a short-term personal loan offered to eligible members of Demo SACCO. It is designed to help members meet personal financial needs such as school fees, household expenses, medical costs, or other approved needs.

### Loan Terms

- **Interest Rate:** 3.75%
- **Processing Fee:** 5%
- **Loan Insurance:** 0.75%
- **Repayment Period:** 76 months

The applicable charges and repayment terms are communicated to the member before the loan is accepted and disbursed.

## Who Qualifies

A member may qualify for a Mgodi Personal Loan if they:

- Have an active membership with Demo SACCO.
- Meet the required membership and savings conditions.
- Have sufficient and verifiable income to support repayment.
- Meet the SACCO's affordability and lending requirements.
- Do not have unresolved loan arrears that prevent further borrowing.
- Provide the required identification, income, and supporting documents.

Meeting the eligibility requirements does not automatically guarantee approval. Each application is subject to credit assessment and approval.

## How to Apply

Members can apply through an approved Demo SACCO branch or the official online loan application channel, where available.

The member provides their personal details, requested loan amount, loan purpose, income information, and required supporting documents.

The SACCO assesses the application based on the member's repayment capacity, existing financial obligations, savings, and loan history.

If the application is approved, the member receives the applicable loan terms, including the approved amount, **3.75% interest rate, 5% processing fee, 0.75% loan insurance, and 76-month repayment period**.

The member should review and accept the loan terms before the loan is disbursed.

## Important

Members should understand the interest, processing fee, insurance cost, repayment period, and other applicable charges before accepting the loan.

Members should borrow only an amount they can comfortably repay.

For questions about the loan amount, charges, repayment schedule, or application status, members should contact Demo SACCO through an official service channel.
