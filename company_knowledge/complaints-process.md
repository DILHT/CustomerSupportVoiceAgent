# Customer Complaints Process

## Purpose

Demo SACCO provides a formal process for members to raise complaints about products, services, staff conduct, transactions, charges, loan applications, digital channels, or other service problems.

Members should first report the complaint through an official SACCO channel, such as a branch, customer service desk, telephone channel, email, or approved digital channel.

## How Complaints Are Handled

When a complaint is received, the SACCO records the complaint and provides a reference number where applicable. The complaint is assigned to the responsible department for investigation.

The responsible team reviews the available information, checks relevant records, and contacts the member when additional information is required. The member should receive an acknowledgement and information about the expected resolution time.

Complaints should normally be resolved as quickly as possible. Simple complaints should be handled immediately where practical. Complaints requiring investigation should be resolved within the applicable service-level timeframe.

## Escalation

If the complaint is not resolved within the expected timeframe, or the member is not satisfied with the response, the complaint may be escalated to the relevant supervisor or department manager.

More serious or unresolved complaints may be escalated to senior management and, where appropriate, the Risk and Compliance function.

A complaint involving suspected fraud, financial loss, misconduct, data protection, security, or regulatory concerns should be escalated promptly to the appropriate control function rather than being treated as an ordinary service complaint.

## Final Response

The member should receive a clear response explaining the outcome of the investigation and any action taken. Where a complaint cannot be resolved in the member's favour, the SACCO should explain the reason and, where applicable, provide information about the next available escalation or external complaint-resolution channel.
